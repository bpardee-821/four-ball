# Four-Ball: Idea and Validation Scope

**Status:** Ready for initial validation; broader demand remains unproven.

## Problem

Golfers often reserve scarce tee times before friends can commit. The organizer may pay for all four players, chase responses, and decide whether to invite others or release spots before the course’s cancellation deadline.

**How might we help organizers confirm their preferred group early enough to fill or release remaining spots without penalty?**

## Direction

Create a shared round linked to an existing reservation. The organizer invites preferred friends, tracks responses, and sees the course’s cancellation cutoff in one place.

Preferred friends receive an initial response window. After it ends, the organizer may invite a wider circle; original invitations remain valid while space is available. Tapping **“I’m in”** confirms a spot. Payment is tracked separately and is not required to confirm.

## MVP

- Organizer enters the course, tee time, price, group size, and cancellation cutoff.
- Organizer sets a response deadline before the course cutoff.
- Friends confirm or decline through a shareable link without installing an app.
- The group view shows confirmed players, pending invitations, open spots, and payment status.
- Organizer receives prompts to widen invitations and resolve open spots before the cutoff.
- Confirmations cannot exceed the reservation’s capacity.

## Assumptions to Validate

- **Friends respond sooner than in group texts.** Compare response times across real outings.
- **Organizers do less chasing.** Track follow-up messages and post-round feedback.
- **The invitation rules are clear.** Confirm that invitees understand the response window and that late invitations work only while spots remain.
- **Setup is worth the effort.** Measure whether organizers use the product again without prompting.
- **The need extends beyond one organizer.** Pilot with several golfers and different groups.

The strongest early signal is repeat use paired with fewer follow-ups and earlier resolution of open spots.

## Not in the MVP

- Tee-time search or booking integrations
- Mandatory payment or financing the organizer’s upfront cost
- Public resale or an open-player marketplace
- Automatic reservation changes or cancellations
- Course marketing and player-data products

These add significant operational or integration complexity and are not required to test whether the coordination experience is useful.

## Open Questions

- How should withdrawals and replacement players work?
- How will changes to the original reservation stay synchronized?
- What identity information must an invitee provide?
- Which business model fits demonstrated repeat use?

## First Test

Pilot the invitation and deadline workflow across real outings with a small group of organizers. Establish each organizer’s current response time and follow-up burden, then compare those measures and repeat usage before investing in booking or payment integrations.
